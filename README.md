# Automato.Club

We're the in-house product studio at Digital3x. Some of our apps start with a task we keep doing by hand. Others start when someone brings us a problem they know firsthand. We work with the people who will use the tool to decide what the first version should do.

## The Digital3x campaign pilot

Our marketing team had an article about reviewing five sales enquiries and wanted to turn it into a campaign. We built a tool that keeps the source material alongside the drafts so a reviewer can check what each piece says before it goes out.

For that article, the pilot produced a campaign strategy, three social post drafts, an email draft, two ad angles, and a visual brief. The team saved all eight for review.

## Bring us an idea

**What do you wish you had a tool for?** Think about the last time you ran into the problem. Who was doing the work? How did they handle it? What would a useful first version let them do differently?

A few sentences are enough. [Send your idea to the studio privately](https://automato.club/contact-us) or [open a public GitHub issue](https://github.com/AutomatoClub/AutomatoClub/issues/new/choose).
